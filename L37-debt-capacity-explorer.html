Debt capacity as a real option

Why a firm rationally borrows less than it can — explore the mechanism.

At t=0 the firm picks debt D₀ up to capacity D̄ and earns the net tax shield τD₀. At t=1, with probability p, an opportunity arrives (cost I, NPV G) that can only be debt-financed — fundable only if it kept room. Move the sliders: lever to capacity when the sure shield τI beats the option value pG; otherwise preserve.

Interactive explorer: adjust the net tax shield, debt capacity, investment size, arrival probability and project NPV to see whether the firm levers to capacity or preserves debt capacity, with the resulting firm value and a plot of value against today's debt.
